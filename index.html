The Journey

Rooted in Vrindavan, Singing for the World

Dhruv Ladla is a devotional singer based in Vrindavan, from Khurja, Uttar Pradesh. His music is steeped in the bhakti of Radha-Krishna — bhajans, kirtans and stutis carrying the spirit of the dham to every stage he sings on.

He performs Bhajan Sandhyas, Kirtan Mahotsavs, Ratri Jagrans and private celebrations, and shares his devotional music with followers across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Spotify — where every upload continues the same mission: to make bhakti accessible, beautiful and unforgettable.
